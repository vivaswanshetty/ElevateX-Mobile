# Privacy Policy for ElevateX

**Last updated: August 11, 2026**

Vivaswan Shetty ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ElevateX mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you.

### Types of Data Collected

#### Personal Data
While using our App, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"). Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:
*   Email address
*   Name / Username
*   Profile picture / Avatar
*   Usage statistics (XP, level progress, task completions, and daily streaks)

#### User-Generated Content
We collect data you create within the app, including:
*   Tasks and gigs you publish
*   Posts, wins, and updates shared on the community feed
*   Direct messages and chat media sent to other users

---

## 2. Use of Data

ElevateX uses the collected data for various purposes:
*   To provide and maintain our service
*   To notify you about changes to our App
*   To allow you to participate in interactive features (Tasks, Duels, Chat, Feed)
*   To track achievements, XP ranks, and daily streaks
*   To process task coin transactions via our secure ledger
*   To provide customer support and handle user feedback

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

We may employ third-party companies and tools to facilitate our App. These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose:
*   **Google Sign-In**: Used for secure authentication.
*   **Cloudinary**: Used to securely host user profile pictures and chat media.
*   **Expo Services**: Facilitates updates and core app architecture.

---

## 4. Data Security & Deletion

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Data. You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time directly within the App's **Account Settings** page. Once requested, your profile, posts, messages, and transaction records will be permanently removed from our active databases.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from children without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers.
